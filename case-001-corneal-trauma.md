### Presentation

A patient in their early thirties presented to the trauma center within
two hours of a workshop accident involving a metal fragment, reporting
pain, tearing, and reduced vision in the affected eye.

### Diagnosis

Slit-lamp examination revealed a full-thickness corneal laceration with
a positive Seidel test, confirming active aqueous leakage. Imaging ruled
out an intraocular foreign body.

### Treatment

The patient was taken for emergency primary corneal repair under local
anesthesia the same day, with microsurgical suturing of the laceration.

### Outcome

At the 6-week follow-up, the wound had healed well with best-corrected
visual acuity substantially improved from presentation, and sutures were
scheduled for gradual removal over subsequent visits.
