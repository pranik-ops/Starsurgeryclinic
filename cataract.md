## Overview

A cataract occurs when the eye's naturally clear lens becomes cloudy,
scattering and blocking light before it reaches the retina. This results
in blurred, hazy, or dim vision that worsens gradually over time.

## Causes

- Age-related changes (the most common cause)
- Eye trauma or injury
- Prolonged UV exposure
- Diabetes and certain other systemic conditions
- Long-term steroid use
- Congenital factors (present at birth)

## Treatment

Cataract surgery removes the clouded lens and replaces it with a clear
artificial intraocular lens (IOL). See our [Cataract & IOL Surgery
treatment page](/treatments/cataract-surgery/) for details on the
procedure, lens options, and what to expect.

## When to seek urgent care

If a cataract develops suddenly after an eye injury, or is accompanied by
pain, redness, or sudden vision loss, this may indicate a traumatic
cataract or other urgent condition — contact our trauma line right away
rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
