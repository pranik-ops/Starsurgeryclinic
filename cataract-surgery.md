## What the procedure involves

1. Numbing drops or a local anesthetic are applied — no general anesthesia
   is needed for the vast majority of cases.
2. A small (2–3mm) incision is made and the clouded lens is broken up and
   removed, usually via phacoemulsification (ultrasound-based removal).
3. A folded artificial IOL is inserted through the same incision and
   unfolds into place.
4. The incision is typically self-sealing and requires no stitches.

## Recovery

- Mild grittiness or light sensitivity for 24–48 hours is normal.
- Antibiotic and anti-inflammatory eye drops are prescribed for several
  weeks.
- A follow-up visit is usually scheduled for the next day.
