Dr. Amita Yadav is the lead ophthalmologist at Star Eye Center and General Surgery Clinic in Gurugram, specializing in emergency ocular trauma, corneal reconstruction, and complex cataract management. She leads the clinic's 24/7 emergency trauma response team and has successfully managed over 500 cases of penetrating and blunt ocular injuries, including pediatric trauma cases.

### Education & Training

- MBBS — Delhi University
- MS (Ophthalmology) — Delhi University
- Fellowship, Ocular Trauma — Government Hospital, Delhi

### Clinical Expertise

- Emergency management of penetrating and blunt ocular trauma
- Corneoscleral laceration repair and reconstruction
- Traumatic cataract management and IOL implantation
- Pediatric eye injury management
- Vitreoretinal consultations for posterior segment trauma
- 24/7 emergency trauma line availability

### Philosophy

Dr. Yadav believes in combining cutting-edge surgical techniques with compassionate patient care. Her approach focuses on preserving vision and preventing long-term complications through prompt diagnosis and targeted intervention, with particular emphasis on protecting children's vision to prevent amblyopia.
