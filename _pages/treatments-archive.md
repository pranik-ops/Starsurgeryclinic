## By Treatment Type

### Medical Management
Conservative treatments using medications to manage conditions.

- [Glaucoma Medication Management](/treatments/glaucoma-medication/) — Topical and systemic medications to lower eye pressure
- [Dry Eye Syndrome Treatment](/treatments/dry-eye-treatment/) — Artificial tears, cyclosporine, lifitegrast, and nutritional supplements
- [Corneal Ulcer Treatment](/treatments/corneal-ulcer-treatment/) — Topical antibiotics, antivirals, antifungals with supportive therapy

### Procedural Treatments
Minimally invasive procedures performed in office or surgical center.

- [Glaucoma Laser Therapy](/treatments/glaucoma-laser-therapy/) — SLT laser to improve aqueous drainage
- [Intravitreal Anti-VEGF Therapy](/treatments/anti-vegf-therapy/) — Direct injection into eye to treat macular edema and abnormal vessel growth

### Surgical Treatments
Major procedures performed in operating room under anesthesia.

## Treatment Timeline

**How long does treatment take?**

| Timepoint | What to Expect |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Initial Consultation** | Comprehensive exam; diagnosis; treatment options discussed |
| **First Treatment** | Medication start, laser procedure, or surgery scheduling |
| **Follow-Up (1-4 weeks)** | Assess response; adjust treatment if needed |
| **Stabilization (1-3 months)** | Maximum benefit reached; ongoing maintenance plan |
| **Long-Term (6 months+)** | Regular monitoring; adjust as needed |

## Treatment Selection

**Choosing the right treatment involves:**
- Severity of condition
- Progression rate
- Individual patient factors (age, health, goals)
- Success rates and risk profile
- Patient preferences (surgery vs. medication vs. laser)

Dr. Amita Yadav will discuss all options and help you make informed decisions about your care.
