## All Conditions (Alphabetical)

### Anterior Segment Conditions
- **[Cataract](/conditions/cataract/)** — Clouding of the lens; most common cause of blindness (reversible with surgery)
- **[Corneal Ulcer](/conditions/corneal-ulcer/)** — Serious corneal infection requiring urgent treatment to prevent scarring
- **[Dry Eye Syndrome](/conditions/dry-eye-syndrome/)** — Insufficient or poor-quality tears; increasingly common with screen time

### Glaucoma
- **[Glaucoma](/conditions/glaucoma/)** — Progressive optic nerve damage; leading cause of irreversible blindness; early detection critical

### Posterior Segment/Retina
- **[Diabetic Retinopathy](/conditions/diabetic-retinopathy/)** — Leading cause of vision loss in working-age adults; preventable with good diabetes control

## By Medical Category

### Anterior Segment (Front of Eye)
These conditions affect the cornea, iris, and front chamber of the eye.

- [Cataract](/conditions/cataract/) — Age-related, traumatic, or congenital clouding of lens
- [Corneal Ulcer](/conditions/corneal-ulcer/) — Serious infection requiring 24-7 emergency evaluation
- [Dry Eye Syndrome](/conditions/dry-eye-syndrome/) — Chronic irritation and discomfort

### Posterior Segment / Retina (Back of Eye)
These conditions affect the retina, macula, and vitreous.

- [Diabetic Retinopathy](/conditions/diabetic-retinopathy/) — Diabetes-related retinal vessel damage

### Glaucoma (Optic Nerve)
- [Glaucoma](/conditions/glaucoma/) — Progressive optic nerve deterioration from elevated eye pressure

## 24/7 Emergency Conditions

These require immediate evaluation to prevent permanent vision loss:

- **[Corneal Ulcer](/conditions/corneal-ulcer/)** — Open wound on cornea; risk of perforation within 24-48 hours
- **[Ocular Trauma](/conditions/ocular-trauma/)** — Penetrating or blunt eye injuries
- **[Acute Angle-Closure Glaucoma](/conditions/glaucoma/)** — Sudden severe eye pain with vision loss; medical emergency

## Treatment Options

Most conditions have multiple treatment options. Treatment depends on severity, progression, and individual factors.

- **Conservative:** Lifestyle changes, lubricants, environmental modifications
- **Medical:** Topical or systemic medications
- **Procedural:** Laser therapy, injections, minor procedures
- **Surgical:** Eye surgery (trabeculectomy, cataract surgery, etc.)

View [Treatments & Services](/treatments/) for detailed information on available procedures.
