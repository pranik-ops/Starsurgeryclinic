## When to seek emergency eye care

- Sudden vision loss or a sudden increase in floaters/flashes
- A penetrating or blunt injury to the eye
- Chemical splash in the eye
- Severe eye pain with nausea or vomiting
- A foreign object embedded in the eye
- Sudden double vision after a head injury

## What to do before you arrive

- Do **not** rub or apply pressure to an injured eye.
- Do **not** attempt to remove an embedded object.
- For chemical exposure, flush with clean water for 15–20 minutes and
  call us while doing so.
- Bring any medications, previous eye records, and a list of what
  happened and when.
